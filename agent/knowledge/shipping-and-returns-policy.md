# Astronomy Shop — Shipping & Returns Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-01. This is the authoritative policy document the concierge
should cite when answering shipping, delivery, and returns questions._

## Shipping options

| Method | Cost (orders under $150) | Cost (orders $150+) | Delivery window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $7.99 | Free | 5–7 business days |
| Expedited | $19.99 | $9.99 | 2–3 business days |
| Overnight | $39.99 | $29.99 | next business day if ordered before 1pm ET |

- Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or US
  public holidays begin processing the next business day.
- We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the EU, the UK, Australia, and
  New Zealand. We do not ship to PO boxes for telescopes over 25 lbs.
- Large telescopes (over 40 lbs) ship freight and may require a signature on
  delivery.

## Returns

- Returns are accepted within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund, provided
  the item is in its original, undamaged packaging.
- Opened optical items (eyepieces, filters, imagers) are returnable within
  **30 days** only if undamaged; a 10% restocking fee applies to opened optics.
- Made-to-order and clearance items are **final sale** and cannot be returned.
- To start a return, contact support with your order number. We email a prepaid
  return label for defective items; customer-initiated returns pay return
  shipping.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–10 business days**
  of us receiving the returned item.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error
  or a defective product.

## Warranty interaction

Returns are separate from the manufacturer warranty. For defects discovered after
the 30-day return window, see `warranty-policy.md`.
