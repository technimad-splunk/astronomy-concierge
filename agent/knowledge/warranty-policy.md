# Astronomy Shop — Warranty Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-01. Authoritative source for warranty questions._

## Coverage

- All new telescopes and mounts carry a **2-year limited manufacturer warranty**
  covering defects in materials and workmanship.
- Optical accessories (eyepieces, filters, imagers, Barlow lenses) carry a
  **1-year limited warranty**.
- Consumables (batteries, dew heater strips, cleaning kits) are **not** covered.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects, optical coating failures under normal use, and motor or
  electronics failures in computerized (GoTo) mounts.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, drops, water damage, and damage from improper storage.
- Normal cosmetic wear, and damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Solar observation damage caused by using a scope without an approved,
  full-aperture solar filter. **Never** point an unfiltered telescope at the Sun.

## How to claim

1. Contact support with your order number and a description (photos help).
2. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion; we cover return
   shipping for valid warranty claims.
3. Turnaround is typically 2–3 weeks depending on parts availability.

## Relationship to returns

The warranty is independent of the 30-day return window described in
`shipping-and-returns-policy.md`. Use returns for buyer's remorse within 30 days;
use the warranty for defects that appear later.
