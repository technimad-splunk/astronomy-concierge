# Astronomy Shop — Customer FAQ

_Authoritative answers to common store questions. The concierge should ground
account, payment, and ordering answers in this document._

## Orders & payment

- **Which currencies can I shop in?** The store supports many currencies; use the
  currency tool to list the live set and prices update accordingly.
- **What payment methods are accepted?** Major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard,
  American Express). Cards are charged when the order is placed.
- **Can I change or cancel an order?** Orders can be changed or cancelled within
  1 hour of placement, before they enter processing. Contact support quickly.

## Account & cart

- **Do I need an account to use the cart?** No. Carts are tracked per session;
  the concierge manages your cart for the duration of a conversation.
- **Why did my cart empty?** Carts are session-scoped and may reset between
  visits. Add items again or ask the concierge to re-add them.

## Products

- **How do I know what's in stock and the price?** Always rely on the live store
  tools for catalog, price, and recommendations — these reflect the real
  inventory, not this FAQ.
- **Do you price match?** We do not currently offer price matching.

## Support

- **How do I reach a human?** Email support@astronomy.example with your order
  number. Support hours are 9am–6pm ET, Monday–Friday.
- **Where do you ship and how much does it cost?** See
  `shipping-and-returns-policy.md` for the full shipping matrix and returns rules.
